# NariBot – DPG Compliance Notes

## 1. Open Source & Accessibility
NariBot Core is released under the Apache 2.0 License, enabling reuse, adaptation, and deployment across geographies.

## 2. Platform Independence
The system is designed to operate across low-bandwidth channels including USSD, IVR, and SMS, ensuring accessibility beyond smartphones.

## 3. Privacy & Data Protection
- NariBot minimizes collection of personally identifiable information  
- No sensitive financial or identity data is required for core interactions  
- Data handling depends on deployment context and should comply with local regulations  

## 4. Do No Harm Principles
- NariBot provides guidance, not guaranteed outcomes  
- Avoids high-risk advice (financial, medical, legal)  
- Designed to reduce misinformation through structured flows and constrained prompts  

## 5. Transparency
- Core logic, prompts, and orchestration flows are documented and open  
- External dependencies (LLMs, telecom providers) are clearly indicated  

## 6. Limitations
- Requires integration with external AI and telecom services  
- Performance depends on language models and regional data quality
