# Privacy Policy for NariBot

NariBot is built as a Digital Public Good (DPG) to help women job seekers and entrepreneurs. Our approach to privacy is simple: **Privacy by Design.**

### 1. No Personal Data (No-PII)
NariBot does not collect, store, or process any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). We don't ask for names, phone numbers, email addresses, or government IDs. 

### 2. What we look at
To make the tool better, we only look at anonymized and aggregated data. This means we see "trends"—like which government schemes are popular in a specific language—without ever knowing who is asking.

### 3. Compliance
By staying "No-PII," we naturally align with the following:
* **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023 (India)**: We follow the principles of data minimization and purpose limitation.
* **GDPR**: We use privacy-first architecture to meet international standards.
* **Open Standards**: Our data extraction (JSON/CSV) is transparent and accessible, ensuring no vendor lock-in.

### 4. Third-Party Services
NariBot uses LLM orchestration (like Gemini) to provide responses. We ensure that no personal data is passed to these models. Our prompts are designed to give livelihood guidance based on public datasets only.

### 5. Transparency
Since we build in public, our code and logic are open for audit at any time in this GitHub organization.
